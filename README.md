## Research • NeurIPS 2026 (LP4FM)

### [Can Prompting Reliably Close the Sentence-Length Dispersion Gap?](https://burstiness-research.metaventionsai.com)

**Vittoria Lanzo & Dico Angelo** · *NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Linguistic Principles for Foundation Models (LP4FM) · Poster #114 · Sydney*

[![Research Site](https://img.shields.io/badge/Research_Site_%26_Rhythm_Lab-00d9ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=vercel&logoColor=white&labelColor=0d1117)](https://burstiness-research.metaventionsai.com)
[![OpenReview](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenReview-1E20ig92Zi-00d9ff?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0d1117)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=1E20ig92Zi)
[![Code & Artifacts](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code_%26_Artifacts-00d9ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white&labelColor=0d1117)](https://github.com/burstiness-engine/sentence-length-dispersion)
[![Dataset](https://img.shields.io/badge/HF_Dataset-27K_Generations-00d9ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=huggingface&logoColor=white&labelColor=0d1117)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/burstiness-engine/sentence-length-dispersion)

We formalize sentence-length burstiness as a scale-free point process in word-time using the Goh–Barabási burstiness coefficient with the Kim–Jo finite-$n$ correction ($B_n$), and evaluate **9 frontier model families × 5 prompting modes × 3 seeds (27,000 generations)** against **200 era-, topic-, and register-matched Wikinews articles (2016–2021)**:
- **Prompting moves rhythm; it does not aim it:** Across all 45 model-by-mode cells, **32 land significantly flatter** than human prose, **9 show no significant difference**, and **4 overshoot into higher dispersion**.
- **Few-shot exemplars flatten output:** Supplying median human news exemplars produces flatter text than instruction alone across **9 of 9 model families**.
- **Normalization reverses raw artifacts:** Uniformly stripping markdown scaffolding before scoring overturns an apparent raw instruction-mode equivalence verdict ($d = -0.006$ raw vs. $d = -0.035, p < 10^{-4}$ normalized).
